Love Was
the Recipe.

We preserve the people you love through the food they made for you with love.

Some recipes live on paper. The important ones often live in someone’s hands—how the dough should feel, when the sauce is ready, the ingredient they never measured. Love Was the Recipe is about keeping those things from disappearing.

The recipe is the doorway. The person is the story.

It might be your grandmother’s pierogi. Your dad’s sauce. Your aunt’s salad. Someone else’s mother’s Béarnaise that changed the way you tasted tarragon forever.

The dish doesn’t need to be fancy. It needs to mean something.

We’re building a place to preserve the tactile, unwritten parts of family cooking—the moves, the memories, the stories, and the love that came with being fed.

How it begins

1

2

3

A legacy preserved

The recipe, technique, story, and person become something others can carry forward.

Follow along as Love Was the Recipe takes shape—one dish, one person, one memory at a time.
